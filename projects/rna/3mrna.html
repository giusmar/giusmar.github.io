3′ mRNA-Seq (Digital RNA-Seq)

Gene expression profiling through sequencing targeted to the 3′ ends of transcripts.

Purpose

A form of RNA-Seq that focuses reads on the 3′ ends of polyadenylated transcripts, generally around their 3′UTRs, to estimate gene expression. By sequencing a limited portion of each transcript, it can reduce sequencing requirements compared with full-length RNA-Seq when the goal is to compare gene expression. Actual costs and savings depend on the protocol and experimental design.

Selected Methodological References

  • Sholder G. et al. (2020). 3′Pool-seq: an optimized cost-efficient and scalable method of whole-transcriptome gene expression profiling. BMC Genomics, 21, 64. Describes a 3′-focused gene-expression profiling approach and evaluates its accuracy and scalability.
  • Chen J. et al. (2021). MP3RNA-seq: Massively parallel 3′ end RNA sequencing for high-throughput gene expression profiling and genotyping. Journal of Integrative Plant Biology, 63(7), 1227–1239. Presents a 3′-end RNA-Seq method for gene-expression profiling across many samples.

From Samples to Results

Outputs & Scope

  • Main output: a raw count matrix by gene and sample, suitable as input for differential expression analysis.
  • Quality control: reports for the analysis steps and an aggregated MultiQC report.
  • Downstream analyses: the counts can be used to identify differentially expressed genes (DEGs), followed by pathway enrichment analysis. These analyses require an appropriate experimental design and annotations and are not direct outputs of the workflow.

Quantification is performed at gene level: this workflow is not designed to reconstruct transcripts, analyse isoforms or study splicing. Investigating alternative polyadenylation site usage would require a dedicated objective and analysis.

Technologies
